# Research: Phenopackets v2 / GA4GH Pedigree vs a bespoke pedigree IR

`docs/research/` holds research notes: point-in-time investigations against primary sources that inform a design
decision. They are not living docs; the decision itself lands in `docs/design/`. Date: 2026-09-13.

## Question and answer

Can GA4GH Phenopackets v2 (`Family` / `Pedigree` / `Individual` / `Disease`) serve as grus's meaning-only pedigree IR,
or does grus need its own schema? Also: does the GA4GH Pedigree Standard (Individual + KIN-coded Relationship) do
better? **Answer: neither is sufficient; keep the bespoke IR and add a Phenopackets `Family` exporter/importer.**
Phenopackets' pedigree core is a verbatim PED row
(`family_id, individual_id, paternal_id, maternal_id, sex, affected_status`) \[[pp-pedigree]\] with a single
family-level `consanguinous_parents` bool \[[pp-phenopackets]\]; it has no mating entity, no twins, no adoption, no
pregnancy outcomes, no carrier/presymptomatic, no consultand, no as-drawn labels, no sibling order, no extension slot in
any core message, and it requires per-packet `MetaData` and a CURIE `id` on every disease term \[[pp-metadata]\]
\[[pp-base]\]. The GA4GH Pedigree Standard covers the *edge* vocabulary well (KIN: biological/adoptive/donor/gestational
parent, MZ/polyzygotic multiple birth, consanguineous and separated partner) \[[kin-owl]\] but is a conceptual model
plus a draft FHIR IG (v0.1.0, `ci-build`) \[[fhir-sushi]\]; its only protobuf serialization was a PR that stayed open
three years and was closed unmerged in 2025 \[[pp-pr364]\]. Roughly 60% of grus's field surface has no home in either
(matrix below).

## Reverse direction: what Phenopackets carries that grus lacks

From \[[pp-phenopackets]\] \[[pp-individual]\] \[[pp-disease]\] \[[pp-phenotypic-feature]\] \[[pp-base]\]
\[[pp-metadata]\] \[[pp-interpretation]\]:

- Ontology-coded terms everywhere (`OntologyClass{id CURIE, label}`) with `MetaData.resources` declaring each ontology
  (1..\* required) \[[pp-metadata]\]. A figure gives a legend string, sometimes a gene symbol; no CURIEs. Not relevant
  to a figure-derived IR; relevant to a downstream exporter that wants to look terms up.
- `Disease`: `excluded`, `onset`/`resolution` `TimeElement`, `disease_stage`, `clinical_tnm_finding`, `primary_site`,
  `laterality`. Figures show onset age (covered by `Condition.onset_age`) and occasionally laterality in the legend
  (would be `Annotation PHENOTYPE`). No gap that matters.
- `PhenotypicFeature`: `severity`, `modifiers`, `evidence` (ECO). Figures do not carry these.
- `Individual`: `date_of_birth` (Timestamp), `time_at_last_encounter`, `karyotypic_sex` enum, `taxonomy`.
  `VitalStatus{status, time_of_death, cause_of_death, survival_time_in_days}`. Figures do print `d.72`-style age at
  death; grus currently types it only as `Annotation AGE`, indistinguishable from age at exam. Worth aligning (below).
- `Measurement`, `Biosample`, `MedicalAction`, `File`, `Interpretation` (ACMG class, VRS variation, GENO allelic state).
  Figures carry genotype strings and HGVS strings as text; grus's `GENOTYPE`/`VARIANT` annotations are the right
  granularity for a figure. Structured VRS is out of scope for extraction.
- `MetaData{created, created_by, resources, updates, phenopacket_schema_version, external_references}`; `created`,
  `created_by`, `resources`, `phenopacket_schema_version` are 1..1 / 1..\* \[[pp-metadata]\]. grus `Provenance` is the
  subset that matters; the rest is harness-generated boilerplate.
- Versioning: schema 2.0.0 (approved 2021-06-04, released 2021-10-21) is the only v2 tag; no 2.0.x patch tags
  \[[pp-releases]\] \[[pp-tags]\]. `phenopacket_schema_version` must be `"1.0.0"`, `"1.0"` or `"2.0"` \[[pp-metadata]\].
  The repo now lives at `ga4gh/phenopacket-schema` (old URL redirects) \[[pp-pr364]\].

## What adopting Phenopackets `Family` as the IR would force

- **Required scaffolding per record.** `Family.id`, `Family.proband` (a full `Phenopacket`), `Family.pedigree`,
  `Family.meta_data` are REQUIRED \[[pp-family]\]; every `Phenopacket` (proband and each relative) needs its own `id`
  and `meta_data` \[[pp-phenopacket-docs]\]; each `MetaData` needs `created`, `created_by`, `resources` (>= 1),
  `phenopacket_schema_version` \[[pp-metadata]\]. A trio thus carries three `MetaData` blocks the extractor must emit or
  the harness must inject post hoc.
- **A CURIE for every disease.** `OntologyClass.id` is REQUIRED \[[pp-base]\] and every ontology used needs a `Resource`
  \[[pp-metadata]\]. Legends are free text; the extractor would either invent ids (forbidden by grus's no-inference
  rule), emit a placeholder namespace (non-conformant), or a lookup stage would be required before an IR is valid.
- **One Phenopacket per relative with any data beyond sex/affected.** `Pedigree.Person` carries only the six PED columns
  \[[pp-pedigree]\]; deceased, onset age, genotype text, external id all require promoting the person to a `relatives[]`
  Phenopacket whose `subject.id` equals `Person.individual_id` \[[pp-family]\]. Two parallel lists keyed by string id
  replace grus's single `Individual` list keyed by `Position`.
- **String identity.** `family_id`, `individual_id`, `paternal_id`, `maternal_id` are strings, `"0"` for founders
  \[[pp-pedigree-docs]\]. The as-drawn `I-3` label survives only as a naming convention inside the string.
- **Sex-typed parent slots.** `paternal_id`/`maternal_id` cannot hold two mothers, two fathers, a nonbinary parent, a
  founder sibship, or a childless couple \[[pp-pedigree]\].
- **No extension mechanism.** Neither `Phenopacket`, `Family`, `Individual`, `Pedigree.Person`, `Disease` nor
  `PhenotypicFeature` has an `extensions` field \[[pp-phenopackets]\] \[[pp-individual]\] \[[pp-pedigree]\]
  \[[pp-disease]\] \[[pp-phenotypic-feature]\]; the only `Extension{name, value}` in the v2 tree is on
  `VariationDescriptor` \[[pp-vrsatile]\]. The requirements page defines REQUIRED/RECOMMENDED/OPTIONAL and says nothing
  about extra fields \[[pp-requirements]\]; proto3 JSON parsers reject unknown fields unless told to ignore them, and
  ignored fields are dropped. Every drawing-only fact in the matrix (twins, adoption, pregnancy loss, counts,
  consultand, carrier, separated, childless, `*`, pronouns, panel/gene labels, sibling order) would have to be smuggled
  into `PhenotypicFeature.description` or `Pedigree.narrative`-style free text and re-parsed, which is exactly what
  `ir.md` rejects for `"II-2"`.
- **Emission cost.** proto3-JSON of a `Family` nests `family.relatives[].diseases[].onset.age.iso8601duration` (depth 5)
  and `family.relatives[].meta_data.resources[]` per relative; the same trio in grus's IR is `individuals[3]` +
  `matings[1]`. For an LLM emission surface validated by structured output, the larger and more repetitive the schema,
  the lower first-pass validity (`ir.md`, Alternatives considered).
- **Sex enum footgun.** `Sex` ints are FEMALE=1, MALE=2, the reverse of PED; PR #442 (open, 2026-07) adds warnings
  telling implementers never to use the integers \[[pp-pr442]\]. Relevant to any PED export path.

## GA4GH Pedigree Standard (conceptual model + KIN)

**Model** \[[ped-model]\].
`Pedigree{id 1..1, indexPatients 0..*, individuals 0..*, relationships 0..*, status 0..1, narrative 0..1, date 0..1}`;
`Individual{id 1..1, sex 1..1 (sex assigned at birth), karyotypicSex 0..1, gender 0..1, name 0..1, dateOfBirth 0..1, age 0..1 (Phenopacket TimeElement), populationDescriptors 0..*, deceased 0..1, affected 0..1}`;
`Relationship{individual 1..1, relation 1..1 (KIN term), relative 1..1}`. Relationships are the primary entities; a
"reduced form" recommends parent->child edges downward, sibling/partner edges only when not implied by shared
parents/children, twin edges relative to one individual \[[ped-using]\]. The docs page header says the project is "under
active development" \[[ped-index]\]; the repo has 105 commits \[[ped-repo]\].

**KIN** (Family History Relations Ontology, `http://purl.org/ga4gh/kin.owl`, version IRI `releases/2021-08-05`, FHIR
CodeSystem status `draft`, 49 concepts) \[[kin-owl]\] \[[kin-fhir]\] \[[kin-repo]\]. Object properties relevant to grus:

| KIN:004 / 038 / 006 / 029           | isSpermDonorOf / isOvumDonorOf (both subproperties of biological parent) / isSurrogateOvumDonorOf / isMitochondrialDonorOf | `ReproductiveRole.GAMETE_DONOR` + `parentage = DONOR`      |
| KIN:005 (inverse KIN:039)           | isGestationalCarrierOf                                                                                                     | `ReproductiveRole.GESTATIONAL_CARRIER`                     |
| KIN:022 / 021 / 023                 | isAdoptiveParentOf / isFosterParentOf / isStepParentOf (under KIN:020 isParentFigureOf, KIN:019 isSocialLegalRelativeOf)   | `parentage = ADOPTIVE`, `adoption = IN`                    |
| KIN:007 / 008 / 012 / 054 / 055     | isBiologicalSiblingOf / isFullsiblingOf / isHalfSiblingOf / maternal / paternal half                                       | founder sibship, multiple matings (derived in grus)        |
| KIN:009 / 010 / 011                 | isMultipleBirthSiblingOf / isMonozygotic… / isPolyzygotic… (symmetric, transitive)                                         | `twin_group` + `twin_type` MZ / (DZ, TRIZYGOTIC) / UNKNOWN |
| KIN:026 / 030                       | isPartnerOf / isConsanguineousPartnerOf (symmetric)                                                                        | `Mating` / `Mating.consanguineous`                         |
| KIN:050 / 051                       | isSeparatedPartnerOf / isSeparatedConsanguineousPartnerOf                                                                  | `Mating.status = SEPARATED` (no divorced)                  |
| KIN:013–018, 036, 046, 047, 052–061 | aunt/uncle, cousin, grandparent (maternal/paternal), great-grandparent + inverses                                          | derived, not stored, in grus                               |

Sex is modelled as KIN classes `Sex{Male, Female, UnknownSex, OtherSex}` and `Man`/`Woman` = `Person and hasSex …`
\[[kin-owl]\]. Migration of KIN into the Relations Ontology is being explored \[[ped-kin]\].

**What it covers that Phenopackets does not**: consanguinity per couple, separated partners, childless couples (partner
edge), same-gender/nonbinary parents, twins with zygosity, adoptive/foster/step vs biological vs donor parentage,
gestational carrier, founder sibships, consultand-as-index (undifferentiated), unborn (FHIR only) \[[kin-owl]\]
\[[fhir-unborn]\].

**What it still lacks vs grus**: as-drawn `Position`; nonbinary gender symbol; carrier / presymptomatic / `?`
(`affected` is boolean); per-condition status and inheritance; pregnancy outcomes beyond "unborn" (SB/SAB/TOP/ECT);
collapsed-sibship counts; adoption out; divorced vs separated; childlessness reason; sibling birth order; `*` documented
evaluation; proband vs consultand; typed annotations; panel/gene labels; mating-level annotations.

**Maturity / serializations.**

- FHIR IG: `ga4gh.fhir.pedigree` v0.1.0, `status: draft`, `releaseLabel: ci-build`, FHIR 4.0.1, depends on
  `hl7.fhir.us.ga4gh.phenopacket: dev` \[[fhir-sushi]\]. Profiles: `Pedigree` on `Composition` (sections proband 0..1,
  reason, individuals 1..\*, relationships, other) \[[fhir-pedigree]\]; `PedigreeIndividual` on `Patient` (gender
  identity, unborn, population descriptors, karyotypic sex extensions) \[[fhir-individual]\]; `PedigreeRelationship` on
  `FamilyMemberHistory` with name/sex/born/age/deceased/condition prohibited and a `relative` extension
  \[[fhir-relationship]\]; `AffectedStatus` on `Observation` valueBoolean \[[fhir-affected]\]. The `purl.org` and
  `build.fhir.org` IG URLs both 404 at time of writing. The standard's own text: "will eventually undergo HL7's
  balloting process" \[[ped-using]\].
- Protobuf: the standard's docs link to
  `phenopacket-schema/blob/pedigree/src/main/proto/ga4gh/pedigree/v1/pedigree.proto` \[[ped-using]\]. That branch no
  longer exists on `ga4gh/phenopacket-schema` (404; not in the branch list) \[[pp-branches]\]. PR #364 (opened
  2022-06-23) carried it:
  `org.ga4gh.pedigree.v1.Pedigree{id, narrative, date, status, index_patients: repeated string, individuals: repeated Phenopacket, relationships: repeated Relationship{individual_id, relation: OntologyClass, relative_id}}`;
  closed unmerged 2025-09-26 with "The Pedigree group still hasn't decided on anything, so I'm closing this"
  \[[pp-pr364]\]. Its predecessor PR #362 (2020–2022) was also closed unmerged \[[pp-pr362]\]. The standard's YAML
  examples serialize this never-released message \[[ped-examples]\].
- No JSON Schema. `pedigree-tools` (Node) imports PED/GEDCOM/BOADICEA/"GA4GH Pedigree" and exports GA4GH Pedigree/PED
  \[[ped-tools]\]; `pedigree-validator` validates FHIR JSON with HAPI + optional KIN reasoning \[[ped-validator]\].
- Implementations: Open Pedigree and a CSIRO REDCap plugin (FHIR JSON), Kids First (test), PhenoTips (not fully)
  \[[ped-implementations]\].

**Could it serve as the IR?** No. (1) The only concrete serializations are a draft FHIR Composition bundle (heavyweight,
four resource types per pedigree) or a proto that was never merged. (2) Edge-only modelling has no sibship/mating
entity, so birth order, counts, childlessness reason and per-mating annotations have nowhere to go, and "reduced form"
is advice, not a constraint, so one pedigree has many equivalent encodings, which is hostile to golden diffs and to
structured-output emission. (3) `affected` is a boolean. (4) Every edge needs a CURIE. The *edge vocabulary* is the part
worth borrowing.

## Recommendation

**(b): keep the bespoke IR; add a Phenopackets `Family` exporter and importer, lossless where the target has a home,
fail-loud on required fields the figure cannot supply.** Not (a): the matrix shows the following grus concepts would be
lost outright under Phenopackets `Family`:

`Position` labels; `consultand`; `GENDER_NONBINARY` and `sex_assigned_at_birth`; `CONDITION_STATUS_CARRIER`,
`PRESYMPTOMATIC`, and the drawn-`?` vs not-indicated distinction; `Condition.inheritance`; verbatim `onset_age`;
`documented_evaluation`; every `ReproductiveOutcome` except LIVE; both `ReproductiveRole`s; `count` /
`count_unspecified`; `Annotation` PRONOUN, CHOSEN_NAME, non-sex KARYOTYPE, OTHER (GENOTYPE/VARIANT survive only inside
an `Interpretation`); sibling birth order; `Mating` as an entity, and with it same-gender/nonbinary parents, founder
sibships, childless couples, `status`, `childlessness`, `consanguineous` on any couple other than the proband's parents,
mating `annotations`; `twin_group` / `twin_type`; `parentage`; `adoption`; `Label` PANEL / GENE / PHENOTYPE / OTHER;
`PedigreeSet`.

Not (c) GA4GH Pedigree: no released serialization; boolean affected; edge-only.

**Exporter sketch (grus -> Phenopackets `Family`).** `Family.id` \<- FAMILY label or `Pedigree.id`; `Family.proband` \<-
the `proband` individual (fail loud if none; the schema requires one); `Family.pedigree.persons` \<- one `Person` per
individual: `individual_id` \<- `external_id` if present else `"I-3"`-style from `Position`; `paternal_id`/`maternal_id`
\<- from the mating whose `offspring` contains the child, assigned by partner `gender` MAN/WOMAN (fail loud on two
same-gender or nonbinary parents: no home); `sex` \<- gender by the assume-aligned rule; `affected_status` \<- the
sole/first condition's AFFECTED/UNAFFECTED, else MISSING; `Family.consanguinous_parents` \<- `consanguineous` of the
proband's parents' mating; `relatives[]` \<- a `Phenopacket` for each individual with `deceased`, `conditions` beyond a
bare status, `onset_age`, or annotations; `Disease.term.label` \<- `Condition.name`, `term.id` \<- caller-supplied
mapping or fail loud; `Disease.excluded` \<- UNAFFECTED; `Disease.onset` \<- best-effort ISO8601 parse of `onset_age`;
`vital_status` \<- `deceased`; `alternate_ids` \<- `external_id`; `MetaData` \<- `Provenance` (+ harness `created`,
`created_by`, `resources`, `"2.0"`). Everything in the "lost" list above is reported, not silently dropped.
`Family.pedigree` is byte-equivalent to the PED export `ir.md` already plans, so that path is shared.

**Importer sketch (Phenopackets `Family` -> grus).** Group `Person` rows by `(paternal_id, maternal_id)` to form
`Mating`s (`"0"` -> absent partner); `Position` synthesized (generation = topological depth, index = row order) and
flagged as synthesized in `Provenance`; `sex` -> `gender` (assume-aligned); `affected_status` ->
`Condition{name:"", status}`; `Family.proband.subject.id` -> `proband`; `consanguinous_parents` -> the proband's
parents' `Mating`; `relatives[].diseases[]` -> `conditions` (`term.label` -> `name`, `excluded` -> UNAFFECTED, `onset`
-> `onset_age` as the ISO string); `vital_status.DECEASED` -> `deceased`; `alternate_ids[0]`/`subject.id` ->
`external_id`.

**Alignment to adopt under (b)** — semantics and names to borrow so the exporter is mechanical:

1. Cite KIN codes on grus enum members (comments, not fields): `Parentage.BIOLOGICAL`=KIN:003, `ADOPTIVE`=KIN:022,
   `DONOR`=KIN:004/038; `ReproductiveRole.GESTATIONAL_CARRIER`=KIN:005, `GAMETE_DONOR`=KIN:004/038;
   `ZygosityType.MONOZYGOTIC`=KIN:010, `DIZYGOTIC`/`TRIZYGOTIC`=KIN:011, `UNKNOWN`=KIN:009; `Mating.consanguineous`
   =KIN:030; `RelationshipStatus.SEPARATED`=KIN:050. Gives a fixed mapping for a future GA4GH/FHIR export.
1. Consider KIN's edge-placed donor semantics for the `ir.md` open question on 3-party ART: `Parentage` on the
   `Offspring` edge already carries `DONOR`; adding `GESTATIONAL` to `Parentage` (an edge from the carrier to the child)
   would resolve genetic != gestational != rearing the way KIN does, and make `ReproductiveRole` derivable. Only worth
   doing if the corpus shows ART.
1. Age at death. Phenopackets separates `time_at_last_encounter` from `VitalStatus.time_of_death`; grus types both as
   `Annotation AGE`. Add `Individual.age_at_death` (verbatim string, like `onset_age`) or an
   `ANNOTATION_TYPE_AGE_AT_DEATH` so `d.72` is not conflated with `42y`. Low cost, exporter needs it.
1. Keep `gender` as the primary symbol axis and `sex_assigned_at_birth` as the secondary. The GA4GH Pedigree
   `Individual.sex` is literally "sex assigned at birth", so grus's field name already aligns; Phenopackets `Sex` is
   phenotypic sex and is mapped, not aligned.
1. `Condition.name` stays free text; do not add an ontology id field to the IR. A `term.id` is a lookup product, not a
   figure fact; the exporter takes a mapping.
1. `external_id` -> `Individual.alternate_ids` / `Pedigree.Person.individual_id`; `Provenance.doi` ->
   `ExternalReference.id = "DOI:…"`, `figure_uri` -> `ExternalReference.reference`. No renames needed.
1. `Condition.status UNAFFECTED` is exactly `Disease.excluded = true`; keep the per-condition list (Phenopackets agrees
   that unaffected is per disease, not per person).
1. Never emit PED-style integer sex; use enum names (PR #442) \[[pp-pr442]\].
